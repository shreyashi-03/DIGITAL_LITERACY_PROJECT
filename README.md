## 🔐 Task 5: Cybercrime Awareness
Studied common cyber threats:
- Phishing  
- UPI fraud  
- Online scams  

### ⚠️ Key Learnings:
- Scammers manipulate users rather than hack systems  
- Entering UPI PIN always means **sending money**  
- Importance of verifying links and messages  

### 🛡️ Prevention Tips:
- Avoid unknown QR codes  
- Do not share OTPs or personal details  
- Stay alert on social media
